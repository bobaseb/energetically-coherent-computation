\section{Neural Light Cones\index{neural light cones}}

The concept of\index{neural light cones!as boundary conditions of consciousness}neural light cones provides a powerful framework for understanding how\index{conscious experience}conscious experience maintains coherence while remaining bounded by\index{physical constraints}physical constraints. Drawing inspiration from\index{relativistic physics!analogy to neural light cones}relativistic physics, where light cones define the possible\index{causal relationships!in neural light cones}causal relationships between events in spacetime,\index{neural light cones!defining spatial and temporal boundaries}neural light cones describe the regions of the brain that can maintain\index{causal connectivity}causal connectivity and\index{energetic coherence!maintenance across neural networks}energetic coherence within the\index{temporal windows!for conscious processing}temporal windows required for conscious processing~\cite{zheng2025unbearable, van2025processes}.

Just as a light cone in\index{relativistic physics!light cones in}physics determines which events can causally influence each other given the speed of light, a\index{neural light cones!causal constraints of}neural light cone defines the\index{spatial boundaries!of neural light cones}\index{temporal boundaries!of neural light cones}spatial and temporal boundaries within which brain regions can achieve the\index{coherent energy dynamics}coherent energy dynamics necessary for conscious experience~\cite{frankel2011}. This bounded region is determined by several factors: the\index{neural signal propagation!speed constraints on conscious integration}propagation speed of neural signals, the maintenance of\index{energetic coherence}energetic coherence, and the\index{thermodynamic constraints!on information integration}thermodynamic constraints on information integration~\cite{attwell2001, parrondo2015}.

Within a neural light cone,\index{energy flows}energy flows must maintain sufficient coherence to support\index{conscious processing}conscious processing. This requires a delicate balance between stability and adaptability, achieved through the continuous interaction of multiple mechanisms.\index{astrocytic networks!role in synchronized activity patterns}Neurons and astrocytes within the cone maintain\index{synchronized activity patterns!in neural light cones}synchronized activity patterns, while\index{gap junctions!role in rapid signal propagation}gap junctions and\index{synaptic connections!role in rapid signal propagation}synaptic connections enable rapid\index{signal propagation!maintaining energetic coherence}signal propagation that preserves informational and energetic coherence~\cite{pinotsis2023cytoelectric, pinotsis2023vivo}. The\index{neural light cones!dynamic boundaries of}boundaries of the neural light cone are not fixed but dynamic, shifting based on\index{metabolic conditions!effect on neural light cone boundaries}metabolic conditions,\index{attention!effect on neural light cone boundaries}attention, and\index{task demands!effect on neural light cone boundaries}task demands.

Critically, the neural light cone concept helps explain why\index{consciousness!fundamental limitations of}consciousness cannot extend arbitrarily across space and time. Just as physical causality is limited by the speed of light,\index{conscious integration!speed limitations of}conscious integration is limited by the speed of neural signal propagation and the maintenance of energetic coherence~\cite{dehaene2011}. This explains why, for instance, widely separated brain regions cannot achieve direct conscious integration without intermediate processing steps, and why consciousness requires a\index{temporal windows!minimum requirements for}minimum temporal window for integration.

The neural light cone framework also provides insight into the structure of conscious experience. Regions within the cone can maintain the coherent energy dynamics necessary for conscious processing, while areas outside the cone may support cognitive function without directly contributing to conscious experience~\cite{baars2013global}. This\index{neural light cones!selective inclusion in consciousness}selective inclusion in consciousness is not arbitrary but follows directly from the physical constraints on maintaining energetic coherence across neural networks.

The neural light cone concept extends beyond simple spatial and temporal boundaries to encompass the organization of energetic coherence itself. Within each cone,\index{multi-scale coherence!in neural light cones}multiple scales of organization—from molecular interactions to regional activation patterns—must maintain alignment to support conscious processing~\cite{freeman2007, breakspear2005dynamics}. This\index{multi-scale coherence!alignment for conscious processing}multi-scale coherence is achieved through\index{triangulation!achieving multi-scale coherence through}triangulation, where different regions within the cone maintain stable relationships through continuous\index{mutual feedback}mutual feedback and adjustment.

A key feature of neural light cones is their role in maintaining\index{synchronic unity!of consciousness}\index{synchronic unity!relationship to neural light cones}synchronic and\index{diachronic unity!of consciousness}\index{diachronic unity!relationship to neural light cones}diachronic unity—the integration of conscious experience across space and time respectively. Synchronic unity emerges from the capacity of regions within the cone to achieve\index{synchronic unity!simultaneous coherence in}simultaneous coherence, while\index{diachronic unity!stable propagation of coherent states}diachronic unity depends on the stable propagation of coherent states across successive temporal windows~\cite{bayne2010unity}. This dual unity is maintained through the continuous operation of\index{dynamic feedback loops}feedback loops that stabilize energy flows while allowing for adaptive changes in response to new inputs.

The boundaries of neural light cones are partially determined by\index{thermal noise!thresholds determining light cone boundaries}thermal noise thresholds, which act as natural limits on the maintenance of coherent energy states~\cite{faisal2008, mcdonnell2011}. Regions within the cone must maintain sufficient energetic coherence to overcome these thermal fluctuations, while areas that cannot sustain such coherence effectively fall outside the\index{conscious field}conscious field. This creates a natural selection mechanism for conscious participation, where only those regions capable of maintaining appropriate energetic stability contribute to conscious experience.

Importantly,\index{neural light cones!overlapping fields of}neural light cones are not isolated structures but form\index{overlapping light cones}overlapping fields of influence across the\index{cortical sheet!overlapping light cones across}cortical sheet. This overlap allows for\index{overlapping light cones!smooth transitions in conscious experience}smooth transitions in conscious experience, as different regions enter and exit the\index{conscious field!regions entering and exiting}conscious field based on attention, task demands, and metabolic conditions~\cite{kelso2012}. The interaction between overlapping light cones basically creates a\index{global light cone!from overlapping light cones}global light cone—a broader field of\index{global light cone!as broader field of conscious integration}conscious integration that nonetheless remains bounded by the\index{global light cone!physical constraints on}physical constraints on neural coherence.

The implications of the neural light cone framework extend beyond explaining current conscious experience to inform our understanding of fundamental limitations in consciousness. For instance, the model provides clear reasons why\index{consciousness!uploading limitations}consciousness cannot be "uploaded" or transmitted across arbitrary distances—such processes would necessarily break the continuous, causally-connected energy flows that the light cone maintains~\cite{landauer1996}. Similarly, it explains why consciousness differs fundamentally from\index{sleep!versus conscious states}sleep states, where energy coherence patterns are altered but not destroyed, versus\index{death!versus sleep states}\index{death!irreversible loss of coherent energy maintenance}death, where the capacity for coherent energy maintenance is irreversibly lost.

The neural light cone also helps resolve long-standing questions about the relationship between\index{conscious and unconscious processing!dynamic interface between}\index{conscious and unconscious processing!fluid boundary of}conscious and unconscious processing. Rather than positing a rigid boundary between conscious and unconscious states, the model suggests a dynamic interface where regions move in and out of conscious awareness based on their ability to maintain coherent energy states within the light cone's boundaries~\cite{dehaene2011}. This fluid boundary allows for the brain's remarkable ability to shift attention and integrate new information while maintaining a stable conscious field.

Perhaps most significantly, the neural light cone framework\index{local and global aspects!unified through neural light cones}\index{neural light cones!role in bridging local and global aspects}bridges local and global aspects of conscious experience. By describing how local coherence patterns can align and integrate within broader fields of conscious awareness, it provides a physical basis for both the\index{conscious experience!unity and diversity of}unity and diversity of conscious experience~\cite{da2024mathematical}. This integration occurs through mechanisms of\index{mutual recursion!mechanisms of}\index{mutual recursion!role in integrating local and global experience}mutual recursion and continuous feedback, allowing for\index{coherent diversity!in conscious experience}\index{coherent diversity!local patterns within global unity}coherent diversity—the capacity to maintain distinct local patterns while contributing to a unified global experience.

The implications extend to understanding phenomena such as\index{synaesthesia!as altered state of consciousness}\index{synaesthesia!neural light cone patterns in}synaesthesia and other\index{altered states of consciousness!and neural light cones}altered states of consciousness, where unusual patterns of integration within the neural light cone may lead to non-typical conscious experiences~\cite{hunt2024electromagnetic}. The framework suggests that such phenomena emerge from modifications to the normal boundaries and integration patterns of neural light cones, rather than from purely computational or representational changes.

Understanding how neural light cones enable both local processing and global integration leads naturally to consideration of the\index{temporal dynamics!of consciousness}temporal dimensions of consciousness—specifically, how the brain maintains both moment-to-moment awareness (synchronic unity) and continuous experience across time (diachronic unity)~\cite{bosl2025dynamical}. This theoretical foundation provides a bridge between the physical mechanisms of neural processing and the\index{phenomenological features!bridge to physical mechanisms}\index{phenomenological features!of conscious experience}phenomenological features of conscious experience, suggesting new approaches to investigating both normal and altered states of consciousness.

The neural light cone framework thus provides a critical foundation for understanding one of the most profound mysteries of consciousness: how the brain achieves a unified conscious experience despite its distributed processing architecture. While neural light cones establish the physical boundaries within which coherent conscious states can be maintained, the question remains how the brain leverages these constraints to create our remarkably integrated experience—what philosophers and scientists have long referred to as the "unity of consciousness." This unity manifests in two critical dimensions:\index{synchronic unity!integration across space}synchronic unity, the integration of diverse conscious contents across space at a single moment, and\index{diachronic unity!continuity across time}diachronic unity, the continuity of consciousness across time. These two forms of unity, which might initially appear to require different mechanisms, can both be understood as emergent properties of the coherent energy dynamics operating within neural light cones. By examining how the brain achieves these complementary aspects of conscious unity, we can further develop our understanding of consciousness as a physically grounded phenomenon that nonetheless transcends simple computational descriptions.
