Understanding coherence through ECC thus reveals fundamental principles about how consciousness emerges from physical processes while remaining grounded in rigorous mathematical formalism. The resulting synthesis bridges \index{phenomenology}phenomenology and physics through careful attention to the organizing principles that enable conscious experience.
