This question leads us to digital twins and cyber-physical systems—technologies that maintain bidirectional relationships between physical entities and their computational representations. Unlike traditional computational systems that operate in isolation from physical dynamics, digital twins and cyber-physical systems must continuously synchronize computational models with physical reality. This creates a unique context for examining energetic coherence across the physical-digital boundary. The challenges of maintaining accurate representations while respecting thermodynamic constraints illustrate how ECC principles might manifest in hybrid systems spanning both computational and physical domains—potentially revealing insights into conscious-like processing that emerges not from purely physical or purely computational substrates, but from their coherent integration.
