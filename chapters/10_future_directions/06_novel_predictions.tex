\subsection*{Molecular and Cellular Predictions}

\index{transcriptomic profiles}Transcriptomic signatures represent one of ECC's most distinctive predictions. The framework proposes that consciousness-supporting brain regions should exhibit:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Specific~\index{gene expression patterns}gene expression patterns related to energy management and cellular coherence~\cite{Zeng2017}

    \item Significantly greater~\index{transcriptomic diversity}diversity in their~\index{rich alphabet}molecular alphabets than those processing information unconsciously~\cite{Tasic2018, Gallegos2020}
\end{itemize}

Unlike theories that focus primarily on information processing, ECC uniquely predicts that this transcriptomic diversity will correlate specifically with the capacity for maintaining energetic coherence rather than with general computational demands. \index{single-cell RNA sequencing}Single-nucleus RNA sequencing technologies enable precise testing of these molecular predictions~\cite{Lake2016, Girdhar2025}, providing a crucial empirical test that distinguishes ECC from purely functional accounts of consciousness.

At the cellular level, ECC makes unique predictions about the mechanisms supporting conscious processing:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \index{dendritic computation}Dendritic computation plays a critical role in generating coherent energy states through its nonlinear processing capabilities~\cite{Gidon2020, Beniaguev2021}

    \item \index{astrocytic networks}Astrocytic networks form a crucial component of consciousness-supporting mechanisms rather than merely playing supportive roles
\end{itemize}

These predictions contrast with neuron-centric theories by emphasizing how consciousness emerges from the coordinated activity of diverse cellular populations beyond just neuronal signaling.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Integration across scales:} Unlike theories that focus on a particular level of analysis, ECC generates integrated predictions spanning molecular, cellular, network, and phenomenological domains

    \item \textbf{Distinctive markers:} The theory's emphasis on energetic coherence provides potentially unique signatures of consciousness that could be detected across diverse experimental contexts
    
    \item \textbf{Explanatory power:} ECC accounts for phenomena that challenge purely computational theories, such as the specific effects of anesthetics, the role of field effects, and the importance of non-neuronal cells
\end{itemize}

These comparative advantages position ECC as a framework that could potentially unify diverse empirical observations while generating distinctive predictions that differentiate it from other approaches.
